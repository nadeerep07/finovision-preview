Specialized Tax Practice

VAT & Corporate Tax Support

We provide full-scope corporate tax planning, Federal Tax Authority (FTA) compliance, and Value Added Tax management for mainland, free zone, and multinational enterprises across the UAE. We safeguard your commercial entity through rigorous document hygiene, proper supply classification, and strategic tax return filings.

Led by certified holders of the Diploma in GCC VAT Compliance (PwC / ATT) and US Certified Public Accountants (CPA), our practice supports clients on Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP 0% vs 9%) status, Small Business Relief (SBR) up to AED 3,000,000, Transfer Pricing documentation, and UAE Ministry of Finance Federal E-Invoicing integration.
