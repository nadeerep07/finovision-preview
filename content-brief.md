## Concept-specific content emphasis

### Concept A Premium Corporate

Lead with accounting, audit, internal controls and senior expertise. Use restrained navy and gold styling, professional business photography and concise credibility statements.

### Concept B Modern Minimal

Lead with clarity, connected finance and technology. Use a black-and-white interface, large typography, simplified process graphics and dashboard-inspired visual elements.

### Concept C Modern Advisory

Lead with growth, accessible support and the combination of accounting plus business systems. Use deep green, bright accents, approachable professional photography and clear service cards.
