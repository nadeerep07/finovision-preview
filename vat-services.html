Scope of Practice

Complete UAE VAT, Corporate Tax & E-Invoicing Solutions

Meticulously examining business transactions to align your enterprise with UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) mandates, optimize tax positions, and prevent severe administrative penalties.

Corporate Tax · 01

Registration & EmaraTax

Mandatory corporate tax registration for all UAE mainland and free zone commercial licenses. Setup on the FTA EmaraTax platform, tax period alignment, and annual return submissions.

Corporate Tax · 02

Free Zone QFZP (0% vs 9%)

Strategic qualification as a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) to benefit from 0% corporate tax on qualifying income, satisfying adequate substance, and monitoring de minimis revenue limits.

Corporate Tax · 03

Small Business Relief (SBR)

Evaluating eligibility for Small Business Relief for businesses with gross revenue under AED 3,000,000, eliminating corporate tax liability and simplifying record-keeping compliance.

Corporate Tax · 04

Taxable Income Adjustments

Calculating taxable net profit by adjusting accounting records for non-deductible expenses, 50% entertainment caps, related-party interest caps, and foreign branch participation exemptions.

Corporate Tax · 05

Transfer Pricing & Audit

Ensuring transactions between related parties adhere to the Arm's Length Principle. Preparing local files, disclosure forms, and coordinating mandatory audited financial statements (mandatory for QFZPs and revenue > AED 50M).

Regulatory Tech · 06

UAE Federal E-Invoicing

Preparing your enterprise for the UAE Ministry of Finance E-Invoicing mandate. Peppol DCTCE integration, structured XML/UBL 2.1 electronic invoices, and ERP synchronization.

VAT Compliance · 07

Supply Classification

Categorizing commercial revenue across standard rate (5%), zero-rated (0% exports, international transport), exempt supplies, out-of-scope transactions, and designated free zone operations.

VAT Compliance · 08

Input Tax Recovery (ITC)

Rigorous analysis of commercial invoices to maximize claimable input VAT, segregating blocked entertainment and motor vehicle costs, alongside Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) imports.

VAT Compliance · 09

VAT Filings & Audit Defense

Timely compilation and submission of periodic Form VAT201 returns via EmaraTax. Pre-submission checks, voluntary disclosures (Form 211), and defense during FTA verification queries.
